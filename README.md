## What's in the demo

- Cadet Jump from a 1200 ft aircraft
- Wait for the green light, then jump
- Deploy your own parachute inside the green zone
- Steer through changing wind to the target circle
- Time your landing action and learn the roll (PLF)
- Injury-avoidance tips after every jump, plus 1-3 star scoring

## In the full version

- Free-Fall Expert mode from 10,000 ft with rings to fly through
- 5 landing zones: desert, open water, forest, snow mountain and farm field, each with its own wind and landing action
- Customisable jump heights, zones and colours

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Touch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Steer | Arrow keys or WASD | On-screen arrow pad |
| Jump / Deploy / Land | Space or Enter | Large action button |

Works in any modern browser on desktop, phone and tablet.

## Tech

Single-file HTML5 canvas game in plain JavaScript. No libraries, no network requests.

## Safety note

This is a fun training simulator for learning the basics. Real parachute training must always be done with a certified instructor.
